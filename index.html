PROJECT CONTEXT

One project.
One complete data story.

THE PROBLEM

How do you turn child-company operational data into governed enterprise analytics?

UDP models that exact flow. Raw customers, products, pricing and orders enter through S3. Databricks processes them through Bronze, Silver and Gold, then aligns the child company's Gold data with a parent-company analytics model.

The goal was not to build a generic ETL demo. The goal was to understand the engineering decisions behind a realistic Databricks data pipeline and be able to explain each layer.

ENGINEERING DECISIONS

The parts worth
discussing in L2.

01

Data quality & standardization

Customer processing handles duplicates, whitespace, casing, city corrections, missing values and ID normalization before trusted data reaches downstream consumers.

DeduplicationNormalizationDQ
02

Product master alignment

Product processing standardizes category values, derives division and variant, and creates a deterministic product_code for downstream joins.

PySparkSHA-256Master Data
03

Messy pricing made analytical

Pricing transformations parse inconsistent date formats, validate numeric prices and handle invalid or negative values before Silver output.

Date ParsingValidationEnrichment
04

Full + incremental fact loads

Orders are processed at daily grain and aggregated to monthly grain. Separate full-load and incremental paths support historical and arriving data.

DeltaIncrementalMonthly Grain
05

Delta + Change Data Feed

Bronze and Silver datasets are written as Delta tables, with Change Data Feed enabled on relevant tables to support change-aware processing.

Delta LakeCDFACID
06

Unity Catalog organization

The project uses the fmcg catalog with Bronze, Silver and Gold schemas to create a clear governed namespace for the pipeline.
